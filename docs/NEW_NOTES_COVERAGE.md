# Coverage of Lectures 7–13

This is an **incomplete** formalization. All included proof declarations are
checked by Lean, but that does not establish full coverage. “Partial” and
“restricted” below are not proofs of the unrestricted source statements.
No unproved theorem is exported as an assumption or disguised as a definition
of “regularity.” Source corrections are in [NEW_NOTES_AUDIT.md](NEW_NOTES_AUDIT.md).

## All nine numbered theorems

| Source | Status | What is actually established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L7 T1, MLE consistency | **Corrected argmax theorem** | [MLEConsistency](../LectureNotes/MLEConsistency.lean): `argmax_consistency` proves convergence in probability from uniform criterion convergence, a well-separated population maximum, and maximization. `mle_maximizes_average_log` connects a positive likelihood MLE to the criterion. The source's three printed assumptions are insufficient; a uniform LLN and separation are not proved for every parametric density family. |
| L7 T2, asymptotic normality | **Scalar IID theorem under explicit sufficient conditions** | [IIDScoreAsymptotics](../LectureNotes/IIDScoreAsymptotics.lean): `iid_mle_asymptotic_normality` derives the score CLT, curvature/envelope LLNs, and interior first-order equation from IID observations, derivatives, moment/information identities, and maximization. [MLEAsymptotics](../LectureNotes/MLEAsymptotics.lean) proves curvature control along the random estimation interval, using consistency and an integrable third-derivative envelope. Consistency, positive information, and measurable interior maximizers remain explicit hypotheses. The criterion is a supplied log-likelihood function; normalization and information identities must be checked for each model. The vector theorem is not proved. |
| L9 T1, Neyman–Pearson | **Proved for integrable dominated densities and randomized tests** | [NeymanPearson](../LectureNotes/NeymanPearson.lean): `StatisticalTest.neyman_pearson` and `neyman_pearson_necessity`. The necessity statement is almost everywhere, and the proof handles a zero threshold. `densityPower_eq_power` connects the weighted integral to the model measure, and `density_model_isProbabilityMeasure` proves normalization. Existence/calibration of a threshold is not asserted by these comparison theorems. |
| L9 T2, MLR UMP tests | **Restricted** | [MonotoneLikelihoodRatio](../LectureNotes/MonotoneLikelihoodRatio.lean): `mlr_lower_tail_threshold`, `mlr_lower_tail_power_antitone`, and `mlr_lower_tail_ump` prove composite-null optimality for a calibrated lower-tail rule. Densities are strictly positive, the parameter is ordered, and the cutoff is represented by a data point. General cutoff existence is not proved. The source's strict-monotonicity claim is corrected to nonincreasing power. |
| L10 T1, Wilks | **Scalar IID theorem under explicit sufficient conditions** | [WilksAnalytic](../LectureNotes/WilksAnalytic.lean): `iid_scalar_wilks` proves the χ²(1) limit of twice the maximized log-likelihood difference, using the same explicit IID, derivative, moment, consistency, and interior-maximization hypotheses. The estimator limit and quadratic expansion are derived, not assumed. The remainder bound follows from two mean value inequalities. The constrained multivariate theorem and verification of these sufficient conditions for general density families remain open. |
| L11 T1, Pratt | **Proved** | [ConfidenceSets](../LectureNotes/ConfidenceSets.lean): `pratt_identity` is Tonelli for a jointly measurable confidence graph, permitting infinite expected length. `pratt_ump_confidence_minimal` proves optimal expected volume assuming a UMP test for each singleton null, with a reference measure having null singletons. The claimed two-sided Student-test application is not asserted. |
| L12 T1, posterior normal approximation | **Restricted scalar theorem with explicit regularity** | [PosteriorAsymptotics](../LectureNotes/PosteriorAsymptotics.lean): `centered_posterior_gaussian_limit` proves a uniform bound over all measurable events for the actual centered/scaled posterior. Local quadratic log-likelihood convergence, prior continuity/positivity, and a global integrable envelope are explicit sufficient conditions. The change of variables and normalization are proved. `centered_posterior_gaussian_limit_ae` states almost-sure sampling convergence when these conditions hold almost surely. Gaussian-calibrated local events have asymptotically correct posterior content. [NormalPosteriorLimit](../LectureNotes/NormalPosteriorLimit.lean) verifies the analytic conditions for quadratic normal-mean likelihoods with bounded continuous integrable priors positive at the truth. General IID regularity, vector parameters, and frequentist coverage of estimated-information intervals remain open. |
| L13 T1, posterior-risk minimization | **Proved for measurable minimizers** | [BayesianDecision](../LectureNotes/BayesianDecision.lean): `bayes_risk_eq_expected_posterior_risk` uses the actual posterior Markov kernel and prior predictive distribution; `posterior_minimizer_is_bayes` proves optimality, allowing an exceptional predictive-null set and infinite nonnegative risk. It does not assume existence of a measurable selector for arbitrary action spaces. |
| L13 T2, James–Stein | **Proved for p ≥ 3 and positive variance** | [JamesStein](../LectureNotes/JamesStein.lean): `jamesStein_risk` proves the exact risk formula and `jamesStein_strict_improvement` proves strict improvement at every mean vector. Independent normal coordinates on an arbitrary sample space are covered by `jamesStein_risk_of_independent_normals`. Gaussian integration by parts, the smooth regularized risk, inverse-square integrability via Fatou, and the singular limit via dominated convergence are proved. [SteinDecision](../LectureNotes/SteinDecision.lean) proves finite loss integrals, dominance and inadmissibility for the actual extended-real risk. The separate minimax and positive-part claims remain open. |

contain 214 source theorem declarations and 61 source definitions/structures.

found no forbidden logical assumptions. All thirteen PDFs match the supplied
export byte for byte. Local verification used a clean temporary checkout of
